# Roadmap

Where Speed Reader is headed. Priorities shift as we learn, so treat this as direction, not a promise. Ideas and feedback are welcome in [issues](https://github.com/nikob-dev/speedreader/issues).

## ✅ Shipped

- Highlight text and double-tap **Shift** to start reading
- Click or drag the progress bar to jump anywhere
- Resizable reader with size presets and full-window mode
- **← / →** to skip back or forward about 2 seconds
- **− / +** to fine-tune speed

## 🚧 Now

- **Themes**: light, dark, sepia, high-contrast, or match your system
- **Context on pause**: see the surrounding sentence so you never lose your place

## ⏭️ Next

- **Reading stats**: words read and time saved compared with typical reading speed
- **End-of-read summary**: your speed, time, and any new personal bests
- **Daily goal and streak**: a small habit that tolerates a missed day
- **Resume where you left off** on long articles
- **Cleaner page extraction**: fewer menus and ads in the text

## 💡 Later

- Speed levels you unlock by reading comfortably at a pace
- Gentle speed suggestions based on how often you skip back
- Per-site speed, pacing controls, and multi-word chunks
- Readable fonts, including dyslexia-friendly options
- Stats page with a reading calendar and speed trend
- First-run tutorial and speed calibration
- Speed drills and optional weekly recap

## Principles

- **Private by default**: no accounts, no servers. Your stats sync only through your own Chrome profile.
- **Small and fast**: no remote code, and no new permissions unless a feature truly needs one.
