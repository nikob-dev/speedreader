# Privacy Policy — Speed Reader

_Last updated: 2026-10-13_

Speed Reader does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

- **Page content:** Text you select, or the article text of a page you ask it to read, is processed only inside your browser to display it word by word. It is never sent anywhere.
- **Settings:** Your reading speed and reader size are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome syncs them between your own browsers. The extension's developer never receives them.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no third-party services, no remote code.**

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/nikob-dev/speedreader/issues.
